# OISS architecture film

[![Preview](oiss-preview.gif)](oiss-architecture.mp4)

[Watch the full film](oiss-architecture.mp4) · [Poster](oiss-poster.png)

The film is 2 minutes 43 seconds, 1920 × 1080, with English on-screen explanations
and no audio. It follows one legal eight-instruction example through dependencies,
chain grouping, a 15-cycle optimum, the threshold-DP representation and the measured
15.6 ns implementation. Program cycles and physical evaluation period are distinct.

The example is checked against both released implementations. The explanation
builds from the [architecture](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) and
[mathematical reduction](../docs/MATHEMATICAL_REDUCTION.md).

Animation made with [ManimGL](https://github.com/3b1b/manim).
